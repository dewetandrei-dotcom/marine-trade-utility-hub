Polyester vs. Epoxy: Which should you use?

Epoxy is stronger, waterproof, and bonds better to old fiberglass. Polyester is cheaper and faster to cure. For structural repairs below the waterline, always use epoxy. For cosmetic repairs above the waterline, polyester is often sufficient.

How to prep fiberglass for repair

Grind the area with 24-36 grit sandpaper to create a smooth taper. Clean thoroughly with acetone to remove all wax and grease. Never apply new fiberglass over a shiny surface.
